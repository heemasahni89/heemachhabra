Interior Design Services

Every project is different, so the scope is tailored to the property, client and stage of work rather than treating every service as automatically included.

Residential Interior Design

A home should feel like an expression of the people who live there. HCDC develops residential interiors as a coherent whole — balancing layout, comfort, proportion, materiality, lighting and detail rather than treating rooms as isolated decorative exercises.

  • Living rooms
  • Dining spaces
  • Bedrooms
  • Kitchens
  • Bathrooms
  • Home offices
  • Walk-in wardrobes
  • Entertainment spaces
  • Complete homes

Scope where appropriate: Space planning and layout development; Material and finish selection; Bespoke furniture; Custom cabinetry and joinery; Lighting concepts; Furniture specification; Soft furnishings; Art and accessories; Styling and finishing. Not every service is included in every commission; scope is agreed per brief.

Private Residences

For more comprehensive private-residence briefs, architecture, furniture, lighting, materials, joinery and decorative layers are considered together so that the home develops as one composition. The objective is not luxury for its own sake, but an interior with character, comfort and longevity — tailored to the way its occupants live.

Commercial Interior Design

A commercial interior is part of how a business is experienced. HCDC considers identity, function, circulation, atmosphere, durability and user behaviour together so that the space supports both the organisation and the people using it.

Builder & Property Development Interiors

HCDC works with builders and property developers on interior concepts that balance design identity, buyer appeal, usability and considered specification.

  • Show homes
  • Common areas
  • Spatial planning
  • Material palettes
  • Furniture direction
  • Lighting concepts
  • Finish selections
  • Design guidelines
