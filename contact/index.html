Questions

What type of interior design projects does HCDC work on?
HCDC works on residential interiors and private residences, commercial spaces, and builder or property-development projects. The design scope is tailored to each property, brief and stage of work.
Where is Heema Chhabra Design Consultant based?
HCDC is based in London, United Kingdom. Consultations are arranged by appointment.
What can a residential interior design scope include?
Depending on the brief, residential design can include space planning, layout development, materials and finishes, lighting concepts, bespoke furniture and joinery, cabinetry, furniture specification, soft furnishings, art and styling.
How does an HCDC project begin?
Every project begins with a conversation to understand the property, the people using it, functional requirements, aesthetic preferences, priorities and the current stage of the project.
Does HCDC work with builders and property developers?
Yes. Depending on the project, HCDC can support interior concepts, show homes, common areas, spatial planning, material palettes, furniture and lighting direction, finish selection and design guidelines.
How much does interior design cost?
Interior-design fees depend on the scale, scope and stage of the project rather than a single standard price. Share the project location, property type, areas involved and the support required so that an appropriate scope can be discussed.
When should I contact an interior designer?
It is useful to start the conversation early enough for layout, materials, lighting, joinery and furniture decisions to be considered together rather than resolved separately later in the project.
How can I enquire?
Email info@heemachhabra.com with the project location, type of property or space, what you would like to change and the current stage of the project.
