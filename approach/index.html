The Heema Chhabra Approach

HCDC does not begin with a predetermined signature look. The design language develops from the client, the property and the purpose of the space.

01 — UNDERSTAND

Understand

Every project begins with conversation. We establish how the space is used, what needs to change, the client’s functional requirements, aesthetic preferences and the atmosphere they want to create.

02 — CONCEPTUALISE

Conceptualise

The brief becomes a clear design direction through layout, spatial planning, proportion, colour, materiality, lighting and the overall character of the interior.

03 — CURATE

Curate

Materials, finishes, furniture, lighting, fabrics, artwork and accessories are considered together rather than selected as isolated pieces.

04 — DETAIL

Detail

Joinery, transitions, proportions, lighting, finishes and practical details are resolved from the scale of the room down to the points where materials meet.

05 — EXECUTE

Execute

The design is developed so that key decisions, specifications and details can be carried into implementation with clarity and consistency within the agreed scope.

06 — EXPERIENCE

Experience

A successful interior is measured not only by how it photographs, but by how naturally it supports the people who live, work or spend time there.
