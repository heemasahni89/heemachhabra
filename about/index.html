About Heema Chhabra Design Consultant

Meet Heema Chhabra and discover the client-led philosophy behind HCDC, a London interior design practice focused on considered, personal spaces.

Exceptional interiors are not created simply to be admired. They are created to be lived in, experienced and remembered.

At Heema Chhabra Design Consultant, every brief begins with the people behind the space — how they live or work, what they need, what they value and how they want the interior to feel.

From that understanding, HCDC develops a coherent design through planning, proportion, materiality, light, furniture, joinery and carefully resolved detail.

Beauty With Purpose

Beauty should never come at the expense of function. We consider how a space is used, how people move through it and how it should feel, then resolve proportion, circulation, light, material, texture, furniture, storage and everyday practicality as one connected composition.

The result should feel considered rather than over-designed: refined, personal and capable of enduring beyond a passing trend.

Meet Heema Chhabra

Heema Chhabra is the designer behind Heema Chhabra Design Consultant, a London-based practice focused on considered residential, commercial and development interiors.

Based in London, United Kingdom. Consultations by appointment.
